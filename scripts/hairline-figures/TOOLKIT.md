# Toolkit figure concepts

All four use the landing figures' fixed `Cam(45, 0.5, 1.6)` and world bounds. Their rest poses are intended to read within a 160 px tall strip. The drawings make the section's existing copy concrete without adding labels inside the art.

- **Architecture library:** A wide component rack with three bays. The left bay holds a vented service, the middle a database cylinder, and the right a table card with three rows. The middle bay has a dashed seat. On hover, the database rises from that seat. Its base, cylindrical bands, and top ellipse stay aligned while moving.
- **Connected, annotated diagrams:** Two vented components sit on a thin diagram plate. An elbow connection joins them. Its midpoint has a pin attached to an upright note whose short rule lines identify written detail without words. Hover extends the note upward from that connection; the pin and stem remain attached.
- **Structured AI assessment:** A small design occupies the left of a plate, connected to an upright sheet on the right. Three ruled finding rows are part of the sheet. Hover unfolds one finding from the middle row, keeping its lower edge tied to the sheet.
- **Export and share:** A working view and an empty receiving frame sit on one plate. A small diagram is printed in the working view. Hover lifts a copy, moves it across the plate and seats it in the receiving frame. Both views retain their screen frames and header rule throughout.

Each gesture reverses on pointer leave or focus loss. The shared Hairline clock stops after settling and respects reduced motion. Production art receives input through the existing same-origin parent message bridge.
